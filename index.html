The Hearlie story

A florist's craft became part of Parramore's fabric.

Local neighborhood history traces Hearlie's Flower Box to the early 1970s and founder Hearlie Mae Donaldson.

Donaldson's artistic ability led her into floristry. She trained at Singleton's Flower Shop on Church Street and later worked at Tinker Bell Florist before opening the business that carried her name.

The shop operated from the historic Hankins Building, a Parramore landmark associated with Black doctors, attorneys, civic leaders, and the Orange County NAACP.
